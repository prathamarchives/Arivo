# arivo — the experience thesis (L0)

> why arivo feels the way it does. the thesis is signed; it is not a
> discussion. changes require owner + evidence (see CONSTITUTION.md §gates).

## the thesis

**arivo is a room, not an app.**
**your library is furniture, not rows.**
**your annotations are objects, not records.**

arivo is a personal intellectual environment built around books — library,
shelf, reading room, desk, notebook, research workspace, laboratory, archive.
reading is one relationship with the source, not the boundary of the product.
the user's real loop is acquire → read → mark → question → research → write →
make → connect → reflect → preserve → return → compound, and the product must
make that loop feel like one continuous place.

## the three places

| place | answers | primary object |
|---|---|---|
| **shelf** | what do i own and what is around me? | the book as object |
| **desk** | what am i doing with this source right now? | the source at the center, work around it |
| **archive** | what came out of my work? | evidence of a mind at work |

transitions are physical: a book **pulls forward** from the shelf and opens
into the desk; an artifact **detaches** from its source and enters the
archive with provenance; an archive object **returns** to its source at the
exact passage. the product should feel like one physical place, never a page
router.

## the emotional contract

arivo feels: personal, quiet, intellectual, tactile, editorial, precise,
curious, alive, comfortable, slightly strange in deliberate places.

arivo never feels: corporate, saas, dashboard-first, social-feed, generic
productivity, database-with-css, design-by-committee, dribbble decoration,
"ai app".

the balance is:

> **warm objects, cool architecture.**

## the pencil law

> **the software draws in pencil. the user draws in ink.**

the system is restrained so the user's work can become expressive. system
chrome is quiet, content is strong, user work is expressive, novelty is
contextual. only one thing is normally primary. if everything attracts
attention, nothing does.

## the two temperaments

two authored modes. no third "balanced" mode. study is dead.

- **lab** — serious, monastic, precise. near-white ivory + charcoal. denser
  (×0.90). authority comes from ink, not hue. suppresses environmental
  variation.
- **den** — warm, comfortable, personal. ivory + warm ink, a restrained
  leather accent. slightly looser (×1.10). allows environmental variation.

temperaments are **deltas**, not separate design systems. they may change
color temperature, density, radius within bounds, texture intensity,
atmosphere, motion pace. they never change anatomy, typography family,
iconography, grid, token structure, or the interaction model.

## booklight (den only)

the current book influences the **light** around arivo — never the walls:

```
cover → extract candidates → clamp → desaturate → luminance constrain
     → taste filter → --aura → apply to atmosphere only
```

booklight may touch: ambient wash, cover shadow, progress hairline,
contextual atmosphere. it may never touch: core text, borders, core
surfaces, typography, layout, annotation identity colors. lab ignores
booklight entirely. peak alpha targets the low teens; it must be visually
validated, not treated as sacred.

## the saturation privilege

the environment is neutral — paper, ivory, ink, charcoal, warm stone.
**saturation is a privilege reserved for the user's marks.** the user's
annotations should be among the strongest color on screen. the system never
competes with the user's thinking. annotation identity colors are constant
across all temperaments and lighting states.

## lighting is a separate axis

temperament (lab/den) and lighting (paper/sepia/night) are independent.
lighting conditions are reading atmosphere, not alternate product
identities. they must not be collapsed into a single theme fork.

## the protagonist law

the software is the room, the light, and the workbench. **the book is the
protagonist.** every meaningful object has a home, a context, and a history.
a highlight knows where it came from, what it is attached to, and what it
became.

## the signature order

> **inter speaks for the room. literata speaks for the author.**

the ui voice is the inter direction — neutral, objective, precise,
quiet. typography itself is never the source of personality. the reading
voice is literata and remains sovereign over its own grid. two systems, one
spatial treaty — never one font system.

## what arivo is not

not a book reader with extra features. not a notes app. not a graph app.
not a dashboard. the architecture is swiss; the surfaces are human; the
content is the protagonist.

> **hard skeleton, soft skin.**
